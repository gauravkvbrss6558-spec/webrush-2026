// how this was built

Nobody's life comes as one file.

It arrives in exports: a streaming history, a bank statement, a spending log kept out of habit. Each one is honest but partial — a single instrument in an orchestra it can't hear. This page takes three real, independently-collected datasets and treats them as if they were one person's paper trail, layered chapter by chapter into a single reconstructed decade. The seams are left visible on purpose.

SOURCE 01

The Playlist Years

149,860 individual song plays, timestamped to the second, 2013–2024. What she listened to, and at what hour.

SOURCE 02

The Ledger

2,461 hand-logged household transactions, 2015–2018 — every snack, fare and subscription, with a note in her own words.

SOURCE 03

The Wanderer

1,500 card transactions across 300+ Indian cities, 2022–2024 — including a stubborn trail of flagged, unexplained charges.
